Hi, I’m really excited about this project—out of all the ports currently being released, this is the one I’ve been looking forward to the most. However, I have a 3rd-generation processor that doesn’t support AVX2. I’d like to request that support for these CPUs be added in future versions. The *Breath of the Wild* (Savage Wind) port added this support in its alpha 3 release; even with a slower compatibility mode, the game runs quite stably at 30–40 fps. I believe a similar solution could work here as well. I would be very grateful if you could consider adding this in future updates. Thank you so much for your hard work, and have a great day!

My Setup:

GTX 1650
Xeon E3 1230V2 (I7 3770 Equivalent)
