--- devmachine1 em 2026-10-26T07:05:34Z
You didnt merge update and base game probably.

How I did it was first patching the base NSP with the update NSP and then using hactool/hactoolnet and AI for like commands which decompress the NSPs into NCAs, then decompress these NCAs again into exefs and romfs dumps and merging both the update and base dumps. Here are the hactool commands for decompressing the NSP into NCAs and then into exefs and romfs dumps and then merging them:

```
.\hactoolnet.exe -k prod.keys -t pfs0 --outdir nsp_out "<base+update>.nsp"

.\hactoolnet.exe -k prod.keys --titlekeys title.keys -t nca "nsp_out\<update_file>.nca"
.\hactoolnet.exe -k prod.keys --titlekeys title.keys -t nca "nsp_out\<base_game>.nca"

.\hactoolnet.exe -k prod.keys --titlekeys title.keys -t nca --basenca "<base_game>.nca" --exefsdir exefs_out --romfsdir romfs_out "<update_file>.nca"
```

Honestly, you could also do this with your switch and NXDumptool. You forgot to merge update and base game.
